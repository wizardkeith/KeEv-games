Do not claim strict zero-warning portability or humanoid/mobile readiness.

Useful foundation: more coherent expression and simple readable identity than our rejected v5, restrained palette, compact geometry and a working basic skeleton. Remaining gaps: helmet cap/straight repeated fringe, rigid separate features, mitten hands, stiff apron/skirt, disconnected topology and basic weights. The original remains preferable to the failed geometry attempt. The optional material version is a small lookdev test, not a solution to Japanese-anime likeness. No game/Unity integration, new portable toon export, publication or art acceptance.
